## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an academic, dignified, and contemporary institutional character. It merges the gravitas of collegiate tradition with the efficiency of modern educational software. Tailored for academic platforms, faculty portals, administrative management suites, and student-facing applications, the visual identity conveys trust, scholarly focus, and understated prestige.

The aesthetic philosophy draws upon Modern Academic Editorialism combined with refined functional minimalism. It replaces harsh corporate sterile grays and loud high-saturation accents with rich, grounding burgundy and comforting warm linen surfaces. The visual tone fosters calm concentration, academic authority, and approachable clarity.

## Colors

The palette centers on a disciplined harmony of collegiate wine hues grounded by warm, natural beige and ivory neutrals. Orange and warm-yellow tones are eliminated in favor of nuanced rose-tinted burgundies and parchment-inspired neutrals.

- **Primary (`#6B1D2F` - Bordeaux Deep):** The core institutional anchor. Used for primary CTAs, critical navigational headers, active states, and high-emphasis focal elements.
- **Secondary (`#8A3345` - Soft Burgundy):** A softer, richer crimson used for secondary interactive states, category badges, supporting metadata icons, and subtle structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#42101B` - Dark Crimson Noir):** Deep, almost-black wine tone reserved for high-contrast typography, high-priority alerts, and focused academic headers.
- **Neutral Base & Canvas (`#F9F6F0` to `#EFEAE1`):** Warm alabaster, linen, and soft sand create a tactile, non-glare reading surface that reduces eye fatigue across long study and administrative sessions.
- **Muted Surfaces (`#E8E1D5` / `#DDD5C7`):** Delicately delineated cards, dividing rules, and table headers that eliminate harsh pure black borders.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the singular typographic voice across headlines, body copy, and UI metadata. Its geometric construction brings contemporary precision, while its humanist proportions and generous aperture maintain outstanding legibility across dense educational content such as gradebooks, syllabi, and administrative schedules.

- Headings utilize tighter tracking and heavier weights to communicate authority and structure.
- Body sizes prioritize comfortable line lengths and generous line-height ratios to make sustained academic reading effortless.
- Labels and micro-metadata enforce purposeful tracking and semi-bold weights for rapid scanning in data tables and student dossiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system designed for density without visual clutter:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Maximizes tabular viewing and multi-pane grading workspaces.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px margins. Collapses secondary sidebars into slide-over panels.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px margins. Single-column stacked layouts ensuring thumb-friendly action targets.

Component rhythm adheres to strict 4px/8px incremental steps, preventing ambiguous alignments and guaranteeing predictable vertical flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through tonal layering paired with soft, warm-tinted ambient depth rather than clinical drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Soft lino-sand background (`#F9F6F0`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Workspaces):** Pure warm ivory (`#FFFFFF` to `#FCFAF7`) framed with low-contrast borders in pale sand (`rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Flyouts, Popovers, Modals):** Warm ivory elevated with a dual ambient shadow: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(66, 16, 27, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(66, 16, 27, 0.04)`. The wine tint in the shadow preserves organic warmth and prevents cold muddiness.
- **Flat Contrast:** Outlines are kept soft and restrained, letting chromatic contrast between wine and beige denote clickable zones and state shifts.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft roundedness (`0.25rem` base, scaling to `0.5rem` on cards and larger panels). This creates a tailored, academic aesthetic that feels grounded, precise, and mature. Excessive rounding is deliberately avoided to preserve an orderly, institutional architecture suited for schedules, official documentation, and student records.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled wine (`#6B1D2F`) with crisp ivory text (`#FAF8F5`). Focus rings utilize a 2px offset in soft burgundy tint (`#8A3345`).
- **Secondary:** Warm ivory surface with subtle sand-wine border (`#DDD5C7`), text in `#6B1D2F`. Hover transforms the surface into a soft blush tint (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No background; wine typography with a soft ivory-beige hover highlight.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with light beige/ivory backgrounds (`#FCFAF7`), bounded by faint 1px warm borders (`#E8E1D5`).
- Card headers utilize clean separation rules and `#42101B` headings to cleanly segregate academic modules, lecture rosters, or administrative records.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pale beige background (`#F7F3EC`) that brightens to `#FFFFFF` on focus, paired with an elegant 1.5px `#6B1D2F` focus border. Placeholder text in `#8C857B`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked state defined by `#DDD5C7` perimeter. Checked state filled with `#6B1D2F`, showing an ivory indicator checkmark.
- **Dropdowns & Selects:** Sandstone surface with crisp typography and subtle burgundy caret iconography.

### Chips & Badges
- Status chips (e.g., enrolled, pending, graded) use soft beige-wine pastel backdrops (e.g., `#F4ECEE`) with deep wine text (`#6B1D2F`) to replace traditional high-saturation tags.

### Academic Lists & Tables
- Alternating subtle rows in `#FAF8F5` and `#F5EFEB`. Header rows framed in `#EFEAE1` with uppercase muted labels (`#8C857B`). Hover states highlight rows with an ivory glow.
